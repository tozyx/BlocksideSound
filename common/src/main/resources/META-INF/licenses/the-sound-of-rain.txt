Selected files: rain_sounds_wood_1..3.ogg, rain_sounds_metal_1..3.ogg,
rain_sounds_glass_1..3.ogg, rain_sounds_foliage_1..3.ogg,
rain_sounds_sand_1..3.ogg, rain_sounds_water_1..3.ogg,
rain_sounds_fabric_1..3.ogg, rain_sounds_droplet_1..3.ogg.
Material recordings retain full source durations; plant drips remain short.
Mono Vorbis processing and runtime fades are recorded in the manifest.
The exact original names, hashes, author comments and crop positions are in
META-INF/licenses/blocksidesound-rain-audio-manifest.json.

Additional weather recordings:
rain7..12.ogg retain full source durations and are downmixed to mono so
native world-space positions have directional panning and distance attenuation.
Sheltered versions use a 2800 Hz low-pass and bounded static filter-loss
compensation (target 1.5 dB below dry mono, gain capped at 1.5). These play at
natural speed rather than inheriting vanilla above's half speed. Native timing
and positions remain; walls also select the sheltered profile using a collision
ray. Both custom rain events have volume 0.7. Original supplied files are unchanged.
thunder_sounds_close_1..3.ogg, thunder_sounds_medium_1..4.ogg and
thunder_sounds_far_1..4.ogg remain converted to complete mono recordings.
Exact source comments/hashes, processing and output hashes are in the manifest.
Original source files and REAPER peak caches are not included in the mod jar.

Original recording comments include:
Ivo Vicic — Rain on umbrella (Loop)
Tomas Herudek — Rain on window ambience
Other recording author comments are preserved in the manifest.
The MIT declaration is the upstream project's declaration; separate original
recording sources have not been verified. These sounds are credited as
third-party recordings, not recordings by BlocksideSound.

Current processing:
Material recordings remain separate complete takes, with the existing mono
conversion and linear category calibration. Background rain7..12 are downmixed
to mono for positional playback. Sheltered copies use a 2800 Hz low-pass and
bounded static compensation for filter energy loss, at natural playback speed.
All background takes retain their full durations, without cropping,
concatenation, time stretching or synthesized loops.
Material layers retain their existing staggered runtime overlap; background
samples are dispatched only from native Minecraft weather requests.
Thunder recordings remain complete; plant drips remain short single events.
Exact processing, original comments/hashes and output hashes are in the
packaged manifest. Original supplied source files remain unchanged.
